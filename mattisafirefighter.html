Fayetteville Fire Department

9 stations134 membersISO Class 113,156 calls (2023)Founded ~1870

Mission: Progressive all-hazards agency — fire suppression, EMS, all rescue types, code enforcement, arson investigation, public education.

EMS: BLS first response (non-transport). All engines carry AEDs and basic medical gear.

History: Roots to ~1870, fully career since the 1970s. Station 1 at 303 W. Center St. is on the National Register of Historic Places.

Hiring contact: Battalion Chief Matt Sallee · msallee@fayetteville-ar.gov

Benefits: LOPFI retirement Program II, medical/dental/vision, life/LTD/AD&D.

Age limit: 18–35 at time of testing.

Links: Dept page · History · NTN posting · Google Maps

City of Fayetteville

~101,680 residents2nd largest in ARU of A — 34,000+ studentsWashington County seat

Economy: U of A generates $3B annual economic impact. NW Arkansas Fortune 500 corridor (Walmart, Tyson, J.B. Hunt). One of the fastest-growing metros in the U.S.

Culture: College town, arts/music scene, outdoor recreation, diverse growing population.

Say in your interview: "A growing university city with increasing call volume and diverse community needs is exactly the environment I want to serve and grow in."

Springdale Fire Department

8 stations133 full-timeISO Class 1/1xCivil ServiceTraining center + burn tower

Civil Service: Follows Arkansas Civil Service Law for hiring — formal, structured process. Mentioning this shows you've done your homework.

EMS: All engines respond with EMTs or Paramedics. Paramedic Ambulances carry 1 Paramedic + 1 EMT.

Staffing: Engines and ladders run 3 personnel. All stations staffed 24/7.

Training culture: Dept publicly emphasizes training as a core value — reference this specifically in your interview.

Links: Dept page · Hiring process · Google Maps

City of Springdale

~90,000 residents3rd–4th largest in AR~41.6% HispanicTyson Foods HQ

Diversity: One of AR's most diverse cities — cultural awareness and community connection will matter to this panel.

Economy: Tyson Foods HQ, strong manufacturing base. Part of the NW Arkansas metro (622K+ residents, $40B+ GDP). Population up 95%+ since 2000.

Say in your interview: "Springdale's diverse, fast-growing community is a big reason I want to serve here — every day is different and every interaction matters."
